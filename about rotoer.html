The Shaktiman Rotavator (rotary tiller) is a tractor-mounted agricultural implement manufactured by Tirth Agro Technology Pvt. Ltd., headquartered near Rajkot, Gujarat. It is used for soil conditioning, seedbed preparation, and mixing crop residues. Key Features & Design
• Heavy-Duty Rotor: Equipped with a helical arrangement of boron steel blades (available in L, C, J, or Spike types)
• Transmission Options: Offered in chain or gear drive variants with single-speed or multi-speed gearboxes.
• Durability: Features oil-bath lubricated rotor bearings, multi-lip oil seals to prevent mud/water entry, and a robust powder-coated frame.
• Adjustability: Includes spring-adjusted trailing boards and multi-adjustable depth skids for uniform leveling.

Popular Models & Specifications

• Mini Series: Designed for compact and small tractors (12–25 HP).
• Semi Champion / Regular Plus Series: Built for medium to heavy soils requiring 35–65 HP tractors, featuring multi-speed options and heavy-grade components.
• Tusker / High Clearance Series: Heavy-duty variants engineered to handle tough residues and deep tilling without clogging.

Pricing & Availability

• Price Range: Prices in India generally start around ₹54,000 for mini models and can exceed ₹1,60,000 for large, heavy-duty multi-speed variants, depending on the specific series and working width.
