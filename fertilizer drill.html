The Khedut Seed Cum Fertilizer Drill manufactured by Khedut Agro Engineering in Rajkot, Gujarat, is an automated, tractor-operated agricultural implement used to place seeds and fertilizers into the soil at the same time.
Key Specifications & Performance
• Models: Available in various row configurations like KASCFDI09, KASCFDI11, and KASCFDI13.
• Tractor Power Required: 35 to 55 HP.
• Box Capacity: Around 70 kg combined for seed and fertilizer (MS Galvanized boxes).
• Working Speed & Area: Covers about 2.5 acres per hour (up to 25 acres per day), using roughly 1.25 liters of diesel per acre.
• Machine Weight: Ranges from 310 kg to 390 kg depending on the model.
• Adjustable Depths & Spacing: Drilling depth is adjustable from 20 mm to 100 mm.

Main Benefits

• No Tillage Needed: Allows direct sowing after harvest without extensive seedbed preparation.
• Simultaneous Application: Drops both seeds and fertilizer uniformly at precise depths.
• Resource Saving: Saves fuel, labor time, and seed quantity while increasing overall crop yield.
• Multi-Crop Compatibility: Works for cotton, wheat, peanuts, castor, garlic, maize, cumin, soybean, and pulses using an inclined-plate seeding mechanism.
