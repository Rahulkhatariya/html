Tractor-mounted spray pumps are specialized agricultural systems attached via a tractor's three-point linkage and powered by the Power Take-Off (PTO) shaft or belt drive to deliver high-pressure liquid distribution for large farms.
Core Components and Types
• Storage Tank: Ranging from 200 liters to 1,000+ liters, typically built from durable polyethylene or fiberglass.
• Pumping Units: Commonly utilize Triplex Plunger/Piston pumps (high pressure, durable brass/stainless steel heads) or Diaphragm pumps (ideal for shear-sensitive chemical mixtures).
• Delivery Systems: Available as Boom Sprayers (horizontal folding arms with evenly spaced nozzles for field crops) or HTP/Hose Reel units (permitting hundreds of meters of hose and spray guns for targeted or orchard spraying).

Key Benefits
• Efficiency: Cover large acreage rapidly compared to manual or engine-operated standalone sprayers.
• Uniform Coverage: Consistent pressure delivery reduces chemical waste and optimizes crop protection.
• Dual Function: Many HTP configurations support simultaneous multi-user handguns or wide boom spans depending on field architecture.
